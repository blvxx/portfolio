# Delivery simulation — Support Operations Automation

> **Simulation only.** Northstar Services, its stakeholders, tickets, constraints, review comments, acceptance results and incident records are fictional. This package demonstrates how I structure delivery and communicate trade-offs. It is not a client engagement, employment record or evidence of real teammates.

## Scenario

A fictional maintenance-services business receives incident reports through a shared inbox. Support engineers spend time sorting requests, searching runbooks and writing repetitive first responses. The proposed MVP adds an AI-assisted triage step but leaves all customer communication and operational changes under human control.

## Stakeholder roles in this exercise

- Product Owner: agrees scope, value and release priorities.
- Support Operations Lead: defines queues, severity and escalation rules.
- Security/Privacy Reviewer: checks data minimisation and access.
- Engineer: designs, builds and documents the workflow.
- QA Reviewer: verifies acceptance criteria and regression risks.

These are simulated roles, not named people or real approvals.

## How to assess this evidence

The artefacts show requirements decomposition, prioritisation, risk management, change control, review response, testing and handover. They do **not** replace actual experience working with a team, owning a client budget or carrying production on-call responsibility. Those claims should only be added to a CV when supported by real work.
