# Games Support Casebook

A practical **simulated** casebook for player-facing game support. It demonstrates how to clarify a player's issue, assess urgency, collect safe evidence, communicate clearly, decide when to escalate and leave a useful handover.

> **Portfolio scope:** all players, game names, order references and events in this casebook are fictional. These are practice scenarios, not real customer tickets, employer policies or claims of commercial support experience.

## Support workflow

1. **Acknowledge** the player's problem without blaming them or making promises.
2. **Scope impact** — one player, one platform, a region or a wider service.
3. **Verify safely** using the approved account-verification workflow. Never request a password, one-time code, recovery phrase or full payment-card details.
4. **Collect evidence** — game/platform, region, time and timezone, client version, order reference, error code and reproduction steps.
5. **Check** known incidents, service status and relevant account/transaction records.
6. **Resolve or escalate** within documented permissions; state what happens next and when the player will receive an update.
7. **Close the loop** with the outcome, evidence, remaining risk and a concise internal note.

## Boundaries

The example priorities and response targets are illustrative only; a real support team must follow its own published policy and contractual SLA. Support must not bypass account security, reverse enforcement decisions without authority, promise refunds or compensation without approval, or request secrets from players.
