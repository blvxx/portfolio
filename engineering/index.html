02 / Working method

Evidence before assumptions

The aim is not just to make something pass once. It is to understand why it behaves that way, prove the result and leave clear documentation for the next person.

STEP 01

Reproduce

Define the expected behavior, scope and conditions of the issue.

STEP 02

Investigate

Inspect logs, API responses, data and service dependencies.

STEP 03

Verify

Test the fix with positive, negative and regression scenarios.

STEP 04

Document

Record evidence, root cause, impact and recovery steps.
